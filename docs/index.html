Concat

A corpus synthesizer. Point it at samples, it cuts them into slices, measures each one, and lays them out on a map of timbre. Then you play the map.

36 descriptors per slice 3 play modes: Notes, Map, Mosaic 16 pad clusters corpus files with live reload web Corpus Builder Schwung 1.3.0+
Download latest release Open the Corpus Builder on your Move GitHub

I made Concat because the granular modules on the Move each read one buffer. Concat reads a whole sample library at once: you travel through timbre across many files, or sing into it and hear your voice rebuilt from the corpus.

The idea

A map of sounds, not a timeline

Every slice gets measured: how bright, how noisy, how loud, what pitch, what timbre (13 MFCCs), each as an average and a variation over the slice. That puts every slice at a point in a 36-dimensional space. Concat flattens it to a 2-D map and plays a cloud of grains from the slices nearest your position.

A corpus

One sample, one folder, or a .concat file that gathers many folders and samples, trimmed and filtered.

A position

X and Y on the map, moved by knobs, the LFO, a note (Zone), a held pad (clusters), or your voice (Mosaic).

A cloud

Grains at a density you set, each a slice near the position, never the same one twice in a row, flowing from the last.

Quickstart

First sound in a minute

  1. In Schwung, add Concat to a track. A new Concat loads the first sample of your first Samples folder by itself.
  2. To choose your own: open Corpus from the root menu, select Source (knob 1), press the jog wheel, and pick a sample, or a .concat file from Corpora.
  3. Scope (on Mod & Set) decides what a picked sample means: File (default) makes that sample the whole corpus, cut into many slices; Folder takes its whole folder.
  4. Wait for Analyze to reach 100 %. A big folder takes a minute the first time; after that it loads from the cache.
  5. Play the pads. Each pad plays a different part of the corpus.
  6. Open View: see the map, and turn the jog wheel through the Recipes. Try Cloud, then Drone.
Next: build a corpus from several folders in the Corpus Builder, trim the samples, hear its map in the browser, and load it onto the Move from there.
The View page

The corpus on the Move's screen

Each dot is a slice. The ring is your position and radius; the small trail is the last slices played. The recipe name and its number sit on top; the bottom line names the knob you touch or says what the analysis is doing.

Knobs on View

X Position, Y Position, Radius, Density, Grain Size, K Nearest, Smooth, Filter: everything you need to move around without leaving the map.

Status line

analysing 3/40 while it works, then nothing. If something went wrong: out of memory, kept old, bad JSON: ..., kept old, 2 sources missing. A failed load never stops the corpus that is playing.

Play modes

Three ways in

Notes (default)

Polyphonic: each pad is a voice with its own envelope. Note Match: Zone puts each pad somewhere else on the map, at natural pitch. Pitch picks the nearest-pitched slice and retunes it. Off plays the current position, retuned.

Map

Move the position with X/Y, or let Follow do it (LFO, or Sequence on the MIDI clock). Hold a pad to jump to one of 16 timbre clusters; let go to come back.

Mosaic

Concat listens to the line/mic input about every 23 ms and answers with the nearest slices. Beatbox into drums, hum into field recordings. Dry Mix blends your input in.

Pads & MIDI

Hands on

Pads

Notes: up to 16 held notes, each with its own envelope, sharing up to 128 grain voices.

Map: hold a pad to jump to a timbre cluster (cluster = note number mod 16). The last pad pressed wins; let go to return.

Pressure goes where the Pressure param says; velocity scales the level.

MIDI

Pitch bend transposes ±2 semitones. MIDI clock drives Follow = Sequence. CC 123 releases all notes. Poly aftertouch and channel pressure both work.

Recipes

Eight starting points

On the View page, the jog wheel browses them. A recipe sets play and sound parameters and never changes your corpus.

Open the Corpus Builder move.local 
http://move.local:7700/api/remote-ui/module-assets/concat/builder/app.html

It is also one click away in schwung-manager's Remote UI: Concat's track has an Open the Corpus Builder button. If your Move answers on another name or address, replace move.local with it.

The page lives on the Move, served by schwung-manager, so the link above only works when your computer can reach the Move. It needs Concat 0.2.0 installed.

Build a corpus

  1. Gather. Drag folders and samples from the Library onto the corpus column, or use +. Ctrl+A selects every sample in a folder; drag them all, or press Add. ↑ ↓ step through samples and play each one.
  2. Listen and prune. A folder arrives collapsed: open it (▸) to hear its samples one by one and leave some out (✕, + brings them back). Only / Skip take name patterns like *Kick*.
  3. Trim. Click a sample: drag on its waveform to keep only that part. Click a slice to hear just that slice. For a sample inside a folder, Trim on its own pulls it out first.
  4. Slice. Onsets or fixed grains, min and max length, gate: saved into the corpus file.
  5. Choose descriptors. The Descriptors tab shows each one's range, median and histogram, and how much it shapes the map. Untick what you don't want Concat to listen for.

Hear the map

Two views, like Concat

Learned (3-D; Move view flattens it to the plane Concat uses) or Raw with Concat's X/Y menu. Colour by source, by a descriptor, or by Pad clusters. Click a source and its slices light up; click a dot to hear it.

Play it

Play the map (or P, or Shift+drag): press and move, and grains fire like Concat's Map mode, with Density, Grain and Spread. With Pad clusters, hold a pad in the grid to hear what that pad reaches on the Move.

Record

● Rec (or R) records what you play. Download the WAV, or save it to Recordings/Corpus Builder on the Move and add it straight back into the corpus.

Size it

The summary shows slices, files and length, and a meter against Concat's Pool Cap: it tells you the Pool Cap to set when the corpus is bigger than 128 MB.

Save and load

Save (Ctrl+S) writes UserLibrary/Corpora/<name>.concat. The header shows which track Concat is on and what it plays. Load on track N saves and makes that track play the corpus, with live progress. After that, every save reloads it on the Move while the old version keeps playing. Unsaved work survives a page reload.

Corpus files

The .concat format

Plain JSON, written by the Builder and readable by hand. Pick one in Source like a sample.

{
  "concat": 1,
  "name": "Night Market",
  "sources": [
    { "path": "Samples/Ahuntsic_Ambiances" },
    { "path": "Samples/MELLOTRON/M300SaxB-20.wav", "start": 0.4, "end": 6.2 },
    { "path": "Samples/DRUMS", "include": ["*Kick*"], "recurse": false }
  ],
  "exclude": ["*click*"],
  "descriptors": { "use": ["loud", "centroid", "flatness", "pitch", "mfcc1-13"] },
  "slicing": { "mode": "onset", "min_ms": 40, "max_ms": 600, "gate": 0.02 }
}
KeyMeaning
sourcesFolders and samples, relative to /data/UserData/UserLibrary. A missing one is skipped and counted.
start · endSeconds: only that part of a sample.
include · excludeFile-name patterns for a folder (*, ?, case ignored). A top-level exclude applies to every folder.
recursefalse keeps a folder to its own files.
descriptors.useloud, centroid, flatness, zcr, pitch, mfcc, mfccN, mfccA-B. Left-out descriptors leave the search, the clusters and the Learned map.
slicingmode, min_ms, max_ms, gate: replaces the Corpus page settings.

Live reload: when the loaded file changes, Concat rebuilds it in the background while the old corpus keeps playing, and only new or changed files are analysed again (a 9170-slice corpus went live in 8.6 s instead of 76 s).

Remote UI

Concat's panel in schwung-manager

In the manager's Remote UI (http://move.local:7700/remote-ui), Concat's track shows a small panel: the corpus playing and its status, a menu of your corpus files to load, and Open the Corpus Builder. Switch Interface to Default at the top of the track for every knob; the manager forgets that choice when the page reloads.

Memory & caches

What it costs

RAM

Pool Cap (128 MB by default, 512 max), checked against free RAM at every load with 350 MB kept for the Move. Too big a corpus keeps an even subset of slices across all of it.

CPU

On a 25,000-slice corpus, under 2 ms per audio block at worst (the budget is 2.9 ms). Analysis runs on its own thread on cores 0 to 2.

Disk

Caches in /data/UserData/schwung/concat-cache: whole corpus (instant reload) and per file (edits). Nothing cleans them yet; delete the folder to reclaim space.

Install

Get it on the Move

  1. Your Move runs Schwung 1.3.0 or later.
  2. Open schwung-manager (http://move.local:7700) and install a custom module from this repo (filliformes/concat-move), or upload concat-module.tar.gz from the latest release.
  3. Power-cycle the Move (Schwung reads module manifests at startup).
Troubleshooting

When it doesn't

You seeDo this
no audio filesThe pick has nothing Concat can read. Pick a sample, not a folder.
out of memory, kept oldLower Pool Cap or trim the corpus.
bad JSON: ...The corpus file has a mistake; the old corpus keeps playing. Save it again from the Builder.
No soundCheck Play Mode and Volume, that Analyze reached 100 %, and that a pad is held (Notes) or the position sits among the dots (Map).
Source shows a numberThe module metadata did not load: power-cycle the Move.
Builder: Concat is not on a trackAdd Concat to a track; the badge updates by itself.
