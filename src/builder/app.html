Keys and tips
Ctrl+Aselect every sample in the library folder
Enteradd the selected library samples
↑ / ↓step through samples (library or corpus) and play each one
→ / ←open / close a folder source
Spaceplay or pause the selected sample
Escstop (back to the start), clear selections, show the whole map
Click a sourceits slices light up on the map
Pmap: switch between rotate and play
Shift + dragmap: play while in rotate mode
Rrecord what you play on the map (again to stop)
Click a slicein a waveform: hear just that slice
Hold a padColour → Pad clusters: hear the cluster that Move pad jumps to in Map mode
Ctrl+Ssave the corpus to the Move

The map shows what Concat navigates. Raw is Concat's default Map Mode (X = Bright, Y = Pitch); Learned spreads the corpus by PCA of the descriptors you keep. Playing the map uses Concat's pick rule: a random slice among the nearest few.

Unused descriptors are left out of the search and of the Learned map. A Raw axis set to an unused descriptor goes flat on the Move too.
